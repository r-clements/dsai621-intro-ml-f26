DSAI 621: Intro to Machine Learning — Interactive Demos

Small interactive apps demonstrating specific topics from the course. Each link opens a standalone page.

  • Bias–Variance Tradeoff with KNN Regression

    Slide K from 1 to 100 and watch twenty KNN fits go from jagged scatter to a smooth, biased bundle, while the bias², variance and test-error numbers update live.

  • Shrinkage: Ridge, Lasso and Elastic Net

    Move λ and watch ridge, lasso and elastic-net coefficients shrink, then drag the OLS estimate to see why the lasso's diamond gives exact zeros, and what happens when two predictors are highly correlated.

  • LDA, QDA, Naive Bayes and Logistic Regression

    Pick a scenario (shared covariance, different covariances, independent features), change the sample size and drag the class means, and watch the LDA, QDA, naive Bayes and logistic boundaries chase the Bayes boundary, with each method's test error in a table.

  • Basis Expansions: Polynomials and Splines

    Switch between polynomial, piecewise-cubic, cubic-spline and natural-spline bases, change the degree or number of knots and drag the knots, and see the fit, its ±2 SE band, the basis functions it is built from, and test error and LOOCV against df.

  • Smoothing Splines

    Set the df of a smoothing spline and watch λ follow and the ±2 SE band widen at the edges, see bias², variance, expected test error and LOOCV against df, and overlay a natural cubic spline with the same df.

  • Decision Trees: Gini, Entropy and Error

    Grow three classification trees, split by Gini, entropy and the error rate, on a four-predictor problem with a wavy class boundary one greedy split at a time, stop them by depth, leaf size, number of leaves or cost-complexity pruning, draw new samples to see how much they change, and see bias, variance, training and test error for every setting of the stopping rule.

  • Generalized Additive Models: Fitting Four Kinds of Term

    Fit a house-price GAM with a smooth age curve, a linear size term, house-type effects and a location map, change n and each smooth's df to see the true and fitted terms move from flat to noisy, and replay backfitting one update at a time as the terms trade the location effect between them.
