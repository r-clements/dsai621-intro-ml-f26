DSAI 621: Intro to Machine Learning — Interactive Demos

Small interactive apps demonstrating specific topics from the course. Each link opens a standalone page.

  • Bias–Variance Tradeoff with KNN Regression

    Slide K from 1 to 100 and watch twenty KNN fits go from jagged scatter to a smooth, biased bundle, while the bias², variance and test-error numbers update live.

  • Shrinkage: Ridge, Lasso and Elastic Net

    Move λ and watch ridge, lasso and elastic-net coefficients shrink, then drag the OLS estimate to see why the lasso's diamond gives exact zeros, and what happens when two predictors are highly correlated.
