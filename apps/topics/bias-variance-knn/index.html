Bias–Variance Tradeoff with KNN Regression

The true function is f(x) = sin(2πx) with noise σ = 0.3, and the n = 100 training inputs x are fixed. Each thin curve is a K-nearest-neighbour fit to a different simulated training set; choose "One fit" to see a single fit drawn through its training data. Drag the slider: small K gives jagged fits that scatter around f (variance); large K gives smooth fits that all miss the peaks in the same way (bias). The bars and the error curves show the exact decomposition, averaged over 2001 test points on [0, 1]. (Lecture notes §4.3; ISL 2.2.2, ESL 7.3.)

Keyboard and screen reader users: Tab to the K slider and use the arrow keys to change K by 1, Shift+arrow or Page Up/Page Down to change it by 10, and Home/End for K = 1 and K = 100. Use the arrow keys in the "Show" group to switch between one fit and 20 fits. The current numbers are announced in the statistics line below the slider, and each chart's description also contains its numbers.
