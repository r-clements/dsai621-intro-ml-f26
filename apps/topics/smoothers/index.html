Smoothers: Smoothing Splines and Local Regression

Both fits on this page are linear smoothers, ŷ = Sy. One slider sets the degrees of freedom df = tr(S). The app finds the smoothing spline's λ that gives exactly that df, and the loess span s = k/n (tri-cube weights on the k nearest points) whose df is closest to it. At matched df the two fits are nearly the same curve. Click the top chart to choose a target point x0. The middle chart shows the weights ℓi(x0) each smoother puts on each yi: its equivalent kernel. Look at the edges. Local-constant loess there averages points that all lie on one side and is badly biased. Local linear removes that bias by giving the far points negative weights, and so does the smoothing spline. The bottom chart is the spline's eigen-picture: Sλ = Σ ρk(λ) ukukT. The two line directions are never shrunk (ρ1 = ρ2 = 1), and wigglier eigenvectors are shrunk harder. λ here is SciPy's: Σ(yi − g(xi))² + λ∫g″², with x on [0, 1]. (ISL 7.5–7.6, ESL 5.4–5.5, 6.1–6.2.)

Keyboard and screen reader users: use the arrow keys on the sliders (Shift+arrow moves 5 steps) and in the radio groups. The "Target point" slider chooses x0, and so do the arrow keys when the top chart has focus (Shift moves 10 points; Home and End go to the ends). The weights at x0 are summarised in the table under the middle chart. Tab to the eigenvalue chart and use Left/Right to step through the eigenvectors; the selected one is drawn below the bars and described in text. The fit statistics table is announced when it changes.

ℓi(x0) is the coefficient of yi in f̂(x0) = Σ ℓi(x0) yi. Both kernels sum to 1. Σℓi(xi − x0) = 0 means the smoother reproduces straight lines, so its bias involves only f″. Bias and SD are exact for this design: E f̂(x0) − f(x0) = Σℓif(xi) − f(x0) and SD = σ‖ℓ(x0)‖ (ESL 6.1.1).

The smoothing spline's eigenvalues ρk(λ) = 1 / (1 + λdk)

Top: how much Sλ keeps of each eigen-direction of the penalty matrix K, for the first 30 (ESL 5.19–5.20, Figure 5.7). df = Σρk. The eigenvectors do not depend on λ; moving the df slider only changes the bar heights. Bottom: the selected eigenvector uk, plotted against xi. Loess has no picture like this: its S is not symmetric.
