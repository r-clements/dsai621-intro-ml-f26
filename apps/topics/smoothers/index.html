Smoothing Splines

A smoothing spline minimizes Σ(yi − g(xi))² + λ∫g″², and its fitted values are a linear smoother, ŷ = Sλy. The slider sets the degrees of freedom df = tr(Sλ), and the app finds the λ that gives exactly that df. Click the top chart to choose a target point x0. The middle chart shows the weights ℓi(x0) the spline puts on each yi: its equivalent kernel, a bump centred at x0 with small negative side lobes. Look at the edges: there all the neighbours lie on one side, and the spline gives the far points negative weights so that it still fits straight lines exactly. λ here is SciPy's, with x on [0, 1]. (ISL 7.5, ESL 5.4–5.5.)

Keyboard and screen reader users: use the arrow keys on the sliders (Shift+arrow moves 5 steps). The "Target point" slider chooses x0, and so do the arrow keys when the top chart has focus (Shift moves 10 points; Home and End go to the ends). The weights at x0 are summarised in the table under the middle chart. The fit statistics table is announced when it changes.

ℓi(x0) is the coefficient of yi in f̂(x0) = Σ ℓi(x0) yi. The weights sum to 1, and Σℓi(xi − x0) = 0 because the spline reproduces straight lines, so its bias involves only f″. Bias and SD are exact for this design: E f̂(x0) − f(x0) = Σℓif(xi) − f(x0) and SD = σ‖ℓ(x0)‖ (ESL 5.4.1).
